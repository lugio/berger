# Code of conduct

## Expected conduct

Treat contributors and users with respect. Discuss ideas and evidence, explain
technical disagreements, accept corrections, and make room for people with
different backgrounds and levels of experience.

Harassment, threats, discriminatory abuse, sexual harassment, impersonation,
and publishing another person's private information are unacceptable.
Do not post credentials or private agent data in community spaces.

This policy applies to project issues, pull requests, reviews and discussions,
and to conduct while representing the project in public.

## Report a concern

No e-mail address is published. Use one of these two ways, which are separate from the vulnerability channel in
[SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md):

- **Report the content.** On GitHub, use **Report content** on the issue, comment or pull request concerned. This reaches the
  repository's moderators without publishing anything.
- **Ask for a private contact.** Open a short public issue with **no detail** (for example "I have a conduct concern, how can I
  reach the maintainers privately?"). A maintainer answers with a private way to continue; share the details only there.

Share only the private information necessary to assess the concern. For a software vulnerability, follow
[SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md) instead.

## Response

Maintainers review reports privately and assess the available evidence.
Depending on severity and repetition, they may request a correction, remove
content, limit participation temporarily, or exclude a participant.
They should explain the decision privately and consider relevant new information.
Reporters and participants must not retaliate against one another.
